# NFTLab Sim

_A hands-on sandbox simulating real NFT use-cases, unlocked through quiz progress_

## Summary

NFTLab Sim combines quiz-based learning with an interactive sandbox where users don't just answer questions about real NFT pilots—they actually mint, transfer, and redeem demo NFTs replicating those scenarios. The first reusable modules cover ticket transfer, one-time visit rewards, and member access. This helps fans, staff, and project teams build confidence before real assets or live operations are involved.

## Target users

- Fans who need a safe introduction before using a wallet or real digital asset
- Venue staff and support teams preparing to explain a new digital experience
- Organizers testing a ticket, loyalty, or membership pilot before launch

## Problem

Reading or quizzing alone doesn't build real confidence in actually using NFTs; people fear making mistakes with real assets.

## Solution

Let users practice real NFT workflows risk-free in a simulated sandbox unlocked progressively via quiz mastery. The same three modules can be reworded and branded for stadiums, arenas, live venues, sports events, attractions, and other entertainment settings without rebuilding the core experience.

## MVP features

- Quiz gates unlocking sandbox scenarios step by step
- Mint/transfer/redeem demo NFTs mimicking real pilot use-cases
- Reusable scenario library: ticket transfer and entry, one-time visit rewards, member access
- Progress tracker showing completed real-world scenario simulations
- Shareable completion certificate NFT after finishing all scenarios

## Business model

- Paid customization of scenario copy, learning content, and visual identity
- Staff training and support-flow design for each pilot
- Pilot launch support, followed by a recurring scenario-library license as the module catalog grows

The initial buyer is a venue, organizer, or implementation partner. Fans use the learning experience without needing to purchase crypto or risk a real asset.

## Why now

Entertainment operators are exploring digital tickets, loyalty, and membership experiences, but adoption depends on more than the technology. Fans need confidence, and frontline teams need a repeatable way to explain and support the experience. NFTLab Sim turns that rollout work into a reusable sandbox.

## Roadmap

- Add more real-world scenario modules based on new pilots
- Introduce social/competitive sandbox challenges
- Explore partnerships to turn simulated scenarios into real pilot onboarding
